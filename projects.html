Projects

Unsupervised projection binning to reduce motion artifacts in free-breathing imaging
A modified K-means clustering with periodic boundary conditions for projection binning, substantially reducing motion and streak artifacts in free-breathing micro-CT. Medical Physics, 2025
AI-driven automated lung sizing from chest radiographs
A framework that trains state-of-the-art segmentation models on chest X-rays and applies computer vision to identify lung-contour feature points for automatic measurement of lung size. Released as a PyPI package. American Journal of Transplantation, 2025
Deep learning for chest radiograph analysis of primary graft dysfunction (PGD)
Segments left/right lungs with SOTA models, then uses representation learning (CNN latent embeddings + PCA) to detect and quantify infiltrates, improving standardized PGD grading and prognosis.
CT-Lung-Analysis: automated DICOM-to-lung-segmentation pipeline
An end-to-end Python tool that converts raw DICOM chest CT to NIfTI, segments lungs without deep learning, and computes air-content maps for rapid quantitative assessment (SimpleITK, nibabel). code
Adaptive filters and equalizers for signal denoising and channel compensation
Real-time interference cancellation and multi-interference rejection (MATLAB) using adaptive IIR notch filters (LMS) and blind/constant-modulus equalization for full digital signal recovery.
