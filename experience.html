As a member of the Functional and Metabolic Imaging Group, my work focuses on advancing imaging techniques for improved pulmonary assessment and lung health monitoring. My research spans several cutting-edge areas:

  • Dynamic Micro-CT Reconstruction: Developing algorithms to minimize motion artifacts in free-breathing micro-CT scans, enhancing image quality and diagnostic accuracy.
  • Novel Functional Lung Mapping: Extracting innovative functional maps through processes like denoising, segmentation, registration, and model fitting, with applications in both CT and MRI for precise lung function analysis.
  • Hyperpolarized Xenon MRI & NMR Spectroscopy: Analyzing NMR spectra to assess gas exchange efficiency in the lungs and reconstructing dynamic 4D isotopic images from hyperpolarized xenon MRI to visualize lung function during free-breathing.
  • AI-Driven Lung Function Assessment: Leveraging computer vision and deep learning to evaluate lung function from chest X-ray images, with a particular focus on post-transplant lung health.
