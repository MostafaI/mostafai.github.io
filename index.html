I am a PhD candidate in Bioengineering at the University of Pennsylvania, in the Functional and Metabolic Imaging Group. My research develops computer vision, signal/image processing, and deep learning methods for medical imaging — spanning MRI, CT, and X-ray — with a focus on advanced imaging and artificial intelligence for optimizing lung transplantation.

I am also an Algorithm Engineer at NeuralTrak, where I work on 2D/3D and tomosynthesis reconstruction and image stitching for volumetric imaging. I hold an MS in Bioengineering from Penn and a BS/MS in Electrical Engineering from Drexel University.
